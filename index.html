01 / About

I'm in my final year of Management Information Systems, and the part of the degree I keep coming back to is the moment a dataset starts answering a real question.

For my graduation project, my team and I surveyed 250 STC customers and interviewed five employees to find where complaint information gets lost between departments. My part was the methodology, the survey analysis and the KPI design, and we built three Power BI dashboards from the results.

Outside that, I've modeled supply decisions with Excel Solver, mapped a restaurant's workflow onto Odoo ERP, and redesigned a retail network's security after the Target breach. I'm looking for an internship where I can do this work on real data.
